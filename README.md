## Evidence and status
The page distinguishes the research prototype, proposed commercial MVP and future farm pilot.
Benchmark values are transcribed from Tables 1–2 of “Real-Time Poultry Mortality Detection: Edge AI Deployment and Benchmarking”, A. N. Moustafa and E. Moustafa, manuscript version reviewed 8 October 2026. Evaluation was controlled/approximate, not an operational commercial farm trial. FPS is not end-to-end notification latency.
Research affiliations and co-authorship do not establish commercial team commitments or institutional endorsement.
